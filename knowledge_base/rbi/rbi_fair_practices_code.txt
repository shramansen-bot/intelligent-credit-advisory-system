1. LOAN APPLICATIONS AND TRANSPARENCY

Loan application processes should provide borrowers with appropriate
information concerning matters that affect their interests.

Relevant fees, charges, prepayment conditions, penalties, interest-rate
related conditions, and other material terms should be disclosed
transparently where applicable.

The objective is to allow borrowers to understand the proposed credit
facility and make informed decisions.


2. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AND PROCESSING

Lenders should have an appropriate system for acknowledging receipt of
loan applications.

Loan applications should be processed within an appropriate and
communicated time frame in accordance with the applicable regulatory
framework and the lender's procedures.

Transparency regarding application processing helps borrowers
understand the status and expected handling of their application.


3. CREDIT APPRAISAL

Lenders should conduct a proper assessment of the borrower's credit
application.

Security or margin requirements should not be treated as a substitute
for appropriate due diligence regarding the creditworthiness of the
borrower.

Credit assessment should therefore consider the borrower's ability and
eligibility to undertake the proposed credit obligation.


4. COMMUNICATION OF LOAN TERMS

When credit is sanctioned, applicable terms and conditions should be
communicated appropriately to the borrower.

The borrower should be informed of relevant terms governing the credit
facility, and the lender should maintain appropriate records of the
borrower's acceptance where required.

Loan documentation should enable the borrower to understand the terms
under which the credit facility is being provided.


5. COMMUNICATION OF CREDIT DECISIONS

Credit applications should be evaluated through an appropriate
appraisal process.

Where applicable under RBI guidance, lenders should communicate
relevant reasons associated with rejection of loan applications.

Clear communication improves transparency and helps borrowers
understand the outcome of the credit-assessment process.


6. DISBURSEMENT AND CHANGES IN TERMS

Loan disbursement should take place in accordance with the applicable
terms and conditions of sanction.

Borrowers should receive appropriate notice of applicable changes in
loan terms and conditions, including relevant changes affecting
interest, charges, repayment, or other material conditions.


7. FAIR TREATMENT OF BORROWERS

Fair lending practices require borrowers to be treated appropriately
throughout the lending relationship.

Lenders should avoid inappropriate interference in the affairs of
borrowers except where permitted under the loan terms or where relevant
new information requires appropriate action.

Recovery activities should not involve undue harassment or coercive
conduct.


8. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

Lending institutions should maintain an appropriate grievance-redressal
mechanism for disputes and borrower complaints relating to lending
activities.

The mechanism should support appropriate review and resolution of
borrower grievances.
