IMPORTANT NOTICE
This file is a concise educational summary of publicly available
Reserve Bank of India regulatory guidance prepared for use in the
Intelligent Credit Advisory System's RAG knowledge base.

It is regulatory context and must not be interpreted as defining the
prototype's internal credit-score, FOIR, risk-classification, or
loan-eligibility thresholds.


1. PURPOSE OF KEY FACTS STATEMENT

The Key Facts Statement framework is intended to improve transparency
and help borrowers understand important terms and costs associated
with a loan before entering into the loan agreement.

For applicable loans, regulated entities are required to provide key
loan information to prospective borrowers in a simple and
easy-to-understand format.


2. APPLICABILITY

The RBI framework applies to retail and MSME term loan products
extended by regulated entities covered by the applicable RBI
instructions.

For this Intelligent Credit Advisory System, the guidance is used as
regulatory context for borrower-facing loan explanations.


3. KEY LOAN INFORMATION

The Key Facts Statement should communicate critical information
associated with the loan.

This includes information needed by the borrower to understand the
financial characteristics and cost of the proposed credit facility.

The information should be presented clearly so that borrowers can
make informed decisions regarding the loan.


4. ANNUAL PERCENTAGE RATE AND COST OF CREDIT

The Key Facts Statement framework requires disclosure of the Annual
Percentage Rate, or APR, as applicable under the RBI framework.

APR represents the annual cost of credit to the borrower and is
intended to provide a more comprehensive representation of borrowing
cost than the interest rate alone.

Applicable charges forming part of the cost of credit should be
appropriately reflected according to the RBI framework.


5. FEES AND CHARGES

Borrowers should receive appropriate information about applicable
fees and charges.

Charges that are required to be disclosed under the applicable RBI
framework should be communicated transparently rather than being
hidden from the borrower.

This supports transparency and allows borrowers to better understand
the overall financial implications of the loan.


6. BORROWER UNDERSTANDING

The Key Facts Statement should be provided in a simple and
understandable form.

The objective is not merely to provide information, but to make
important loan terms and costs accessible to the borrower.

This supports informed financial decision-making and comparison of
credit products.


7. RELATIONSHIP TO THIS PROTOTYPE

The Intelligent Credit Advisory System uses this RBI guidance only as
external regulatory context.

The RBI guidance does not define the prototype's internal minimum
credit score, FOIR thresholds, risk points, product limits, or final
eligibility rules.

Those rules are defined separately in the Synthetic Lending Policy.

The Policy / RAG Agent may retrieve this document when explaining
topics involving transparency, loan costs, borrower disclosures,
charges, APR, or borrower-facing communication.
