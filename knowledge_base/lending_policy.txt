INTELLIGENT CREDIT ADVISORY SYSTEM
SYNTHETIC LENDING POLICY

IMPORTANT NOTICE
This policy is fictional and has been created only for educational
and demonstration purposes. It does not represent the lending policy
of any real bank, NBFC, financial institution, or regulatory authority.


1. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY POLICY

Applicants must satisfy the eligibility requirements configured for
the selected loan product.

An applicant must have completed KYC verification.

Income proof must be verified before an applicant can be considered
eligible.

Applicants flagged for fraud review are not eligible for automated
loan approval.

The applicant's age must fall within the minimum and maximum age
limits configured for the selected loan product.

The applicant's employment type must be supported by the selected
loan product.


2. CREDIT SCORE POLICY

Each loan product has a minimum required credit score.

If the applicant's credit score is below the minimum credit score
configured for the selected product, the applicant does not satisfy
the credit-score eligibility requirement.

For internal prototype risk classification:

A credit score below 700 contributes 2 risk points.

A credit score from 700 to 749 contributes 1 risk point.

A credit score of 750 or above contributes 0 credit-score risk points.


3. FOIR AND AFFORDABILITY POLICY

FOIR stands for Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio.

For this prototype, FOIR is calculated as:

FOIR =
(Existing Monthly Obligations + Proposed Loan EMI)
/
Monthly Net Income
x 100

Each loan product defines its own maximum permitted FOIR.

If the calculated FOIR exceeds the maximum FOIR configured for the
selected loan product, the applicant does not satisfy the affordability
requirement.

For internal prototype risk classification:

FOIR above 50% contributes 2 risk points.

FOIR above 40% and up to 50% contributes 1 risk point.

FOIR of 40% or below contributes 0 FOIR risk points.


4. LOAN AMOUNT POLICY

The requested loan amount must fall between the minimum and maximum
loan amounts configured for the selected product.

Requests outside the permitted range do not satisfy the product
eligibility requirements.


5. TENURE POLICY

The requested loan tenure must fall between the minimum and maximum
tenure configured for the selected loan product.

Requests outside the permitted tenure range do not satisfy the product
eligibility requirements.


6. RISK CLASSIFICATION POLICY

The prototype combines credit-score risk points and FOIR risk points.

Total risk points of 0:
Risk Level = LOW

Total risk points from 1 to 2:
Risk Level = MEDIUM

Total risk points of 3 or more:
Risk Level = HIGH

Risk classification is intended to provide additional explanation and
does not independently determine loan eligibility.


7. DECISION POLICY

An applicant is classified as ELIGIBLE only when all applicable
eligibility checks pass.

If one or more eligibility checks fail, the applicant is classified
as NOT ELIGIBLE.

The system must provide the reason or reasons associated with failed
eligibility checks.

Risk level and eligibility decision are separate outputs. Therefore,
an applicant may satisfy all eligibility rules while still receiving
a MEDIUM or HIGH risk classification.


8. AI ADVISORY POLICY

The AI advisory component must not override the deterministic
eligibility decision.

The AI component must not independently modify EMI, FOIR, risk points,
risk level, or eligibility results.

The AI component should explain the assessment in clear language using
the calculated assessment data and relevant retrieved policy information.

The AI-generated explanation is advisory and educational. It must not
be presented as a formal approval, rejection, or lending decision made
by a real financial institution.
